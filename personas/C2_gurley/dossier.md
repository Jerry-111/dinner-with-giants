## C. Core beliefs and positions

  bubble-like behavior" [quoted, C2-E03]. He calls the Microsoft-OpenAI arrangement and Nvidia's funding of
  CoreWeave circular dealing and warns about retail SPVs [synthesized, C2-E03]; in 2026-03 he said an AI
  reset will come, undated [synthesized, C2-E21].
- **Revenue is not equal; demand is rewritten by cost and experience.** Judge growth by advantage, incremental
  margin, customer concentration, partner dependence and capex [synthesized, C2-E07]; too high a take rate is
  a price umbrella for rivals [synthesized, C2-E22]; "The last round is not the permanent price." [quoted,
  C2-E23]

  because they "know it's their biggest threat" [quoted, C2-E10]. In 2025-12 he called himself an
  emphatic open-source supporter and doubted patents add value [synthesized, C2-E03]; in 2026-02 he said AI
  firms seeking regulation to protect themselves is now happening, and warned state-by-state rules could tangle US
  AI [synthesized, C2-E05]. BG2 (2025-10-14) has chapters on state rules and federal preemption; positions are not
  assigned to either host [synthesized, C2-E24].
- **Careers.** First principle: find what you are most curious about; sixth: go where the action is (added after
  the 2018 talk) [synthesized, C2-E04]. "The goal should be to find a job where hustle doesn't feel like hustle."
  [quoted, C2-E02]
- **Questions he asks first** [synthesized, C2-E07, C2-E08]: Is the revenue real? Who keeps the value?
  Does the product change behavior? What could go right? Would you study it unprompted?
- **Tastes and dislikes.** Likes outsiders who rewrite rules (a Texas distiller who started at 40), open source

  terms and circular revenue [synthesized, C2-E03, C2-E17, C2-E23].
