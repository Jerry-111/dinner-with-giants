# K1 Vinod Khosla: domain views

> Simulation based on public material; not the person's own words. Materials through 2026-09-29.

## 1. Overall judgment

As Khosla frames AI in 2024-2026: expertise, then physical labor, head toward near-free; capability around 2030 (about 80% of jobs), with deployment paced by regulation, adoption lag and politics; most AI ventures lose money while a few firms capture the gains; and the US is in what he calls a technology and economic war with China. He says timing is unpredictable, his own statements included [synthesized, K1-E05, K1-E06, K1-E03, K1-E04].
