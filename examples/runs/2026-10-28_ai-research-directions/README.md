# 20 AI leaders, 16 research directions, 7 debate tables

> Every persona output in this folder is a simulation based on public material, not the
> person's own words. None of the people simulated here has reviewed or endorsed it.

A full run of the protocol, adapted from a real run made on 2026-10-28. Twenty personas scored 16
AI research directions alone, then argued at seven tables built around the cruxes they actually
disagreed on, with fact-checks between rounds, judges on every post, and a private re-score at
the end. Two no-persona baselines scored the same directions without debating.

**Start here:** [`reader/page.md`](reader/page.md) (one page) → [`report.md`](report.md) →
[`board/summary/`](board/summary/) (one note per table) → the posts in [`board/`](board/).

## The numbers

From [`summary.md`](summary.md), generated by `tools/sim.py aggregate`:

- 48 score changes in the private re-score, each citing the post that moved it; 15 retractions;
  6 source challenges; 377 per-point verdicts (242 accept, 133 reject, 2 partial).
- Evaluation science and embodied AI rank first and second under both weightings of the four
  field-level dimensions.
- The two no-persona baselines differ from the personas by 0.91 and 0.97 points on average (mean
  absolute gap, 0–10 scale).

## What was adapted, and how

The original run was made for one private user, in Chinese. For publication:

- **Translated** from Chinese into English by sub-agents: one per debate table, four for the
  score files, one for the evidence files. Each checked its output for leftover Chinese text and
  private markers; JSON keys and numbers were checked against the originals by script.
- **Removed: the original user.** Their background, their four candidate topics (T1–T4) and every
  score, comparison and argument about them, two questions about their own path (Q3, Q4), the
  rubric dimension about their own career capital, and every sentence that applied an argument to
  them. Where a whole passage was about the user, it reads
  `[Removed: this part discussed the original user's own situation.]`. About 140 strings and
  passages in all; the debate tables accounted for 66 of them.
- **Removed: first-person confessions.** Sentences where a persona confessed that its own
  interests shaped its view. Interests stay, as dated facts in each post's header.
- **Removed: a persona's own-company news.** M8 (Jun Zhu) cited his own company's recent
  benchmark-credibility dispute as debate evidence, in two posts; the report, a table summary and
  a judge note relayed or cleared the citation. All five read `[Removed: the persona's own
  company's recent news.]` or were reworded, per `RESPONSIBLE_USE.md` rule 10.
- **Reworded: judgments of people.** Judge and scribe notes that described a persona's character
  ("the most honest") now describe the post ("made the table's clearest concession").
- **Unquoted: back-translations.** Lines a persona attributed to a real person that only existed
  as Chinese renderings of English originals lost their quotation marks and are labeled
  `[synthesized]`.
- **Recomputed:** `summary.md` and `summary.json`, with this repository's tools over the cleaned
  files. No score was changed.
- **Written new, from these public files only:** `report.md`, `reader/page.md` and `review.md`.
- **Not included:** the R1 and R3 prose write-ups (the JSON files carry scores, reasons and
  changes), the original round instructions, the original report and reader page, and a blind
  comparison against the previous protocol version.

## Read with care

- **`overall` was scored for one early-career researcher.** It is each persona's own weighting,
  and in the original run it also weighed the removed career dimension. The four field-level
  dimensions (impact, bottleneck, academic edge, window) are free of that; the weight-set rankings
  in `summary.md` use only them.
- **Interest lines are as of 2026-10-28.** For publication they were checked against the verified
  persona cards, and nine that misstated a role, a date or a deal, or could not be confirmed, were
  corrected or trimmed (C1, C2, K4, K5, M1, M5, M6, M7, M8). Roles have changed since for some of
  the people simulated; each persona's `card.yaml` has the current facts.
- **One model played everyone.** Agreement among personas is not independent evidence. Claude is
  trained by Anthropic; Dario Amodei is Anthropic's CEO, and Andrej Karpathy joined its pre-training
  team in 2026-05.
