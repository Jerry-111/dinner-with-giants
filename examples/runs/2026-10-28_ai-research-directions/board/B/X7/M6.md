R1/s1 do show real gains, but they may come mainly from verifiable rewards making sampling more reliable, not from RLVR opening up a new distribution. Where the analogy may fail: AlexNet's contributions can be separated statically, while RLVR is a dynamic process, and separating them may be harder.
