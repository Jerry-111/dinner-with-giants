# X5 · A opening · Saining Xie (M4)
> Stance from: Rejected twice at top conferences, accepted right after switching venue; ten years of diagnostic-benchmark work, now betting on world models
> Interests: Co-founder and chief science officer of AMI Labs (2026-); bets on JEPA-style world models
> Simulation based on public material; not the person's own words.

**Claim (one line):** Physical AI should bet on D08 (representation / world models) as the upstream foundation, not on D07 (generative action policy) — the policy head is only one implementation on top of the representation, not the end point.

**Argument:** What my ten years of work found: the real bottleneck of downstream capability is representation quality, not the "head" attached on top of the representation. REPA (2024-10) showed that the representations a diffusion model learns on its own have long lagged behind those of models built specifically for self-supervised learning, and that aligning to an external representation speeds up training by more than 17.5x [synthesized, E11] — how well a generative head can do is capped by representation quality. Experience argument: MMVP (2024-01) found that CLIP-style representations are the root of MLLMs' visual shortcomings, not insufficient language reasoning. It taught me that however sophisticated the modules stacked on a representation (reasoning, action, generation), if the underlying representation is wrong, the downstream is doomed to patch a leaky bucket (dossier §C, E06). Applied to D07/D08: the action policy is the "head" and the world model is the "base"; if the base has not learned physical structure, however well the head is designed it just overfits on a bad representation. The analogy may not hold: robot tasks have directly measurable external feedback; if the gradient signal of the action head alone can get the representation right, with no need to learn the representation first, the analogy fails.

**My R1 scores on related directions:** D07 null (no judgment; not my area), D08 8

**What fact would change my judgment:** If someone shows that an action policy trained end to end, without any explicit world model / representation pre-training, matches the route of "learn the representation first, then attach a policy head" in sample efficiency and generalization on complex long-horizon robot tasks, I will change my judgment.
