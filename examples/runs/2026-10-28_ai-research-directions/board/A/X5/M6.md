AlexNet only came afterwards, attaching neural networks and GPUs; I assess my team's contribution at about one third [synthesized, dossier§A/§I, M6-V1-E16]. Where the analogy may fail: OpenVLA-OFT shows that continuous-action regression beats the diffusion head without a structured representation — end-to-end can also approach the structured route. This is the point in my argument that most deserves to be questioned.
