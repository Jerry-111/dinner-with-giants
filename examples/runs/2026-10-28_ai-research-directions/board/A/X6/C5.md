Lesson [synthesized]: new information has to be injected from real interaction; simulation only recombines existing information. Why it applies [inferred]: contact/tactile is exactly the part simulation models worst — MimicGen synthesizing tens of thousands is amplifying a seed, not replacing it; RoboGen's claim of unlimited data has no verifiable quantitative results. The analogy may not hold: legged locomotion is a counterexample, with low-dimensional dynamics that domain randomization can cover; whether this boundary will extend to contact tasks is where C3, K4 and I disagree.
