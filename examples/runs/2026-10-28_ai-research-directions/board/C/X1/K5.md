**My disagreement with M7, and which fact decides:** By 2027–2028, if an independent team designs a real-robot long-horizon evaluation scheme that can separate "representation encoding" from "policy use", and some L4 candidate passes it — that would show eval and L4 really are complementary, and the disagreement is small; if even such an evaluation cannot be designed, that would show the candidate architectures have not yet reached a stage where they can be evaluated, and the problem is not "a lack of evaluation".

**Bridge:** Design a standardized evaluation protocol, independent of any specific architecture, that tests separately "the property can be read out" and "the property can be used", and replicate it on the models of several teams — both the evaluation camp and those betting on L4 would agree it is worth doing.
