# X2 · A opening · Su Shi (H6)
> Stance: Passed the 1057 palace exam, then spent decades administering provinces, twice imprisoned or exiled over memorials and poems written along the way.
> Interests: Song-dynasty court official, 1057-1101; paid by official salary except after 1080, 1094 and 1097, when demotions meant farming granted land instead.
> Simulation based on public material; not the person's own words.

**Claim (one line):** O4 is a chosen bet, not a forced one, only with a real way back into paid work; a human backstop checks the work, not the clock.

**Argument:** After the 1079 Crow Terrace case, demotion to an unpaid post left farming the Eastern Slope at Huangzhou as the only income; that stretch produced the career's most durable work, the Red Cliff rhapsodies and *Calming the Waves* (dossier §G, §I, H6-E02-H6-E04, `[synthesized]`). Lesson: unstructured time can be extraordinarily productive, but provably so only in hindsight; at the time there was no way to know whether a way back existed, and it never fully resolved — a later reversal repeated the pattern at Huizhou and Danzhou. Why it applies: a real "reviewer" was present throughout, a brother, Su Zhe, who received the period's most personal writing (dossier §A, H6-E05, `[synthesized]`) — yet that checked quality, not the length of the exile or its return. A co-maintainer (C3/C5) is the same kind of backstop.

Where this might break for 2026: a backstop could directly create the way back, if that person brings institutional ties or turns the project into paid work, a path this era had no equivalent of. The sides may not be as opposed as framed: a strong co-maintainer could function as the reopenable offer (C4) by another route.

**My R1 scores on the relevant options:** O4 7

**What fact would change my mind:** Confirmation that a named co-maintainer or reviewing mentor is already prepared to convert into a funding or hiring relationship if the year goes well — then it operates as C4 by another name, and this disagreement mostly dissolves.
