# X2 notes: the independent year (O4) -- a reversible exit, or a human backstop?
> Simulation based on public material; not the person's own words.

**Consensus:** A good year that hasn't converted to paid work yet is a different failure mode from a wrong-direction mistake; a backstop addresses one, reversibility the other. A deferral or reopened offer must be actively arranged in advance -- a policy check found it is never a background guarantee. A typical 2026 co-maintainer is a thinner, unbound commitment than the historical partnerships (marriage, kinship) used as backstop examples.

**What's still disagreed:** Whether reversibility (savings plus a reopenable offer) or a human backstop (co-maintainer or mentor) is the more load-bearing safeguard. Whether a part-time volunteer can do what the cited historical backstops did.

**Strongest arguments:** For reversibility -- A/X2/H2: patience through years of unconverted work survived only because income was never at risk. For a backstop -- A/X2/H4: an independent checker catches a wrong direction no exit route can; A/X2/H5: a reviewed project and an unreviewed one of hers diverged, with no deferrable offer in either case.

**The fact that would settle it:** Whether Alex's offer(s) can be deferred or reopened, and whether a named co-maintainer or mentor has already committed.

**What's new:** A pre-committed, dated end to the year (Faraday's interjection), proposed as a safeguard distinct from both sides. A cited burnout statistic was accurate as quoted but drawn from a narrower, self-selected population than stated.
