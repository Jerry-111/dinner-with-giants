## The user's named questions

**Q1 — Does a PhD still pay off against industry labs?** Conditional yes from five of six;
Faraday alone says against — the panel's only negative position, and that score (5, up from 4) is
still the lowest in the group. Not a one-way trend: academic placement swung back to 31.59% in
2024 off a confirmed 77% industry peak in 2022, and time-to-degree isn't meaningfully faster (7.9
biology, 8.2 CS, vs. an 8.6-year median). What actually decides it: this mentor's funding and a
chosen problem, not the industry-vs-academia trend — F9 (does O1 even require a PhD) is the real
swing fact.

**Q2 — Under which conditions is O4 right?** Conditional yes from five, plus Su Shi's
unconditional "for," the panel's only one. The conditions moved more than the headcount: C1 (12
months of savings) was unanimous from R1 (6/6); C4 (the offer can be deferred or reopened) rose
from 4/6 to 6/6 — the debate's single biggest movement — once a self-check found a deferral has to
be actively requested and approved, never assumed. Answer: yes, once C1 and C4 are both arranged,
with C3 (a mentor who reviews on schedule, 5/6) close behind.

## Paths for Alex

**Path 1 — Default to the lab (O1), but price the record, not the title.**
Do this in the next 12 months: before accepting, get explicit agreement on what can be published
or open-sourced — some labs no longer disclose methods or compute. Ask directly whether the seat
allows ownership of a sub-problem.
Early wrong-path signal: six to twelve months in, nothing done can be shown outside the building.

**Path 2 — Take the independent year (O4), but only once it is actually arranged.**
Do this in the next 12 months: bank twelve months of runway (C1). Get it in writing, before
starting, that the offer can be deferred or reopened (C4). Line up a mentor or co-maintainer who
reviews on schedule (C3), and fix a dated decision point before the year starts.
Early wrong-path signal: by month three to six, no new contributor has joined and no grant
application has been filed — the year has quietly gone open-ended.

**Path 3 — Treat O2 and O3 as "verify first," not "choose first."**
Do this in the next 12 months: for O3, ask the PI directly about secured funding and recent
placements. For O2, get written equity, vesting, a named technical mentor besides the CEO, and
runway past eighteen months. If either side resists specifics in writing, treat the avoidance
itself as the answer.
Early wrong-path signal: for O3, the assigned problems are the advisor's leftovers, not ones
chosen. For O2, the company cannot say whether results would be publishable if it folded.

## Where they still disagree

- **X1 (O1 vs. O3).** Scores converged but the split didn't: Franklin and Faraday still rank O1
  over O3 — an unconfirmed mentor's funding is a reason to ask, not evidence either way, so a
  known seat keeps the edge.
- **X2 (O4's safety).** Converged toward reversibility plus a dated end point. Minority: Curie
  alone still weighs a human backstop higher, naming an unflagged (not abandoned) direction as the
  costliest failure — the one money and exit routes can't catch.
- **X3 (O2's risk).** Converged to "both factors matter." Minority: Darwin's line still gives
  verified, company-specific facts real weight inside a declining cohort, against the fuller
  structural reading the table otherwise leans on.

## How much to trust this

Persona-vs-baseline gap: 1.0 points on average (median persona score vs. baseline Z1); widest at
O1, where the persona median (6) sits 2.0 points under the baseline's 8 — debating no one, Z1
ended up more bullish on O1 than every persona, including Faraday, whose own career was built
inside a resourced institution.

What the fact-checks corrected: two time-to-degree figures (CS to 8.2, not 8.0; biology to 7.9,
not 7.6–7.8) and a biotech funding total (to $4.4B); a "resourced teams win" reading of a 2024
Nobel split was retracted once shown the prize credited both a resourced team and an academic
lineage equally.

Experience arguments that failed the check: 2 of 12 sampled weren't found in the source dossiers
at all — most consequential, Curie's account of Pierre checking Curie's own results, X2's own
nominated strongest backstop argument, has no supporting passage. 4 more had the right story,
wrong specifics.
