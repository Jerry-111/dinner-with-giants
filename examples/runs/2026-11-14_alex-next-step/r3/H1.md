## Final advice for Alex

Verify before committing years to either an unverified mentor or an unverified "founding"
title: ask the PI directly about secured funding and where their last student landed; get the
startup's vesting, a named technical mentor besides the CEO, and runway past eighteen months in
writing, knowing a named mentor alone only weakly predicts fundraising success. If the
independent year is the real draw, take it only with twelve months of savings, a deferrable
offer, and a backstop who is actually obligated to check the work, not merely well-meaning — a
typical open-source co-maintainer is a volunteer, thinner than any partnership I ever relied on.
Whatever is chosen, do not give too much for the whistle: a title, a famous name, or a mentor's
promise is worth exactly what it lets Alex point to afterward, not what it sounds like now.
