# Digest (optional step)

Optional. For a small roster, skip this and go straight to synthesis (`protocol/06_synthesis.md`).
Use it when the combined persona material is too large for the lead writer to read end to end:
split the run's areas (domains, options, sub-topics — whatever `scope.md` defines) across
sub-agents, one digest per area.

Each digest uses `templates/run/digest.md` and covers:

1. A tier table: for this area, is each persona first-tier, conditional, downgrade, or no-call —
   including any change after debate.
2. Consensus.
3. Real disagreement: who and who, where it sits, what evidence would settle it.
4. Research questions or options worth backing in this area.
5. Ways of engaging that will be downgraded, and why (absorbed into something larger, automated,
   or commoditized).
6. The 3–5 year and 10 year view.
7. Reversal signals.
8. Key evidence and its fact-check status.
9. Usable quotes, with sources.
10. A digest author's note: where the material is thin, where it conflicts.

Digests feed the lead writer (`protocol/06_synthesis.md`), who still reads `summary.md` and the
full scribe notes directly — a digest summarizes persona material, not the debate mechanics.
