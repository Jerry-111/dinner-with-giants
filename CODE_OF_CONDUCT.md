# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticize arguments rather than people, and keep
discussions about simulated personas clear of attacks on the real people behind them.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's
Security tab ("Report a vulnerability") or the contact listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`. Reports are
handled confidentially.
